## Description

Predictive model estimating risk of homelessness presentation to support early intervention.

## Business Purpose

Prioritise proactive support and prevent avoidable homelessness through targeted outreach.

## Data Sources

* Housing Register Data
* Temporary Accommodation Records
* Council Tax Arrears Flags
* Benefits Processing Data

## Key Outputs

* Risk Score (0-100)
* Predicted 6-Month Presentation Probability
* Primary Risk Driver Category
* Recommended Intervention Tier

## Questions This Answers

* Which households are at highest near-term risk?
* What risk drivers are most influential?
* How should caseload prioritisation be adjusted?
