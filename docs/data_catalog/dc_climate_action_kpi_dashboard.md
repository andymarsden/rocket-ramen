## Description

Strategic dashboard tracking council and borough-wide climate indicators against net-zero commitments.

## Business Purpose

Monitor progress toward emissions reduction targets and coordinate cross-service delivery.

## Data Sources

* Council Estates Energy Consumption Dataset
* Fleet fuel and EV charging data
* Waste and recycling performance data
* Transport model outputs

## Key Measures

* Council Operational Emissions (tCO2e)
* Borough-Wide Emissions Proxy Index
* Renewable Electricity Share (%)
* EV Fleet Share (%)
* Recycling Rate Contribution to Emissions Reduction

## Questions This Answers

* Is the council on track for interim carbon targets?
* Which directorates are reducing emissions fastest?
* Which interventions have highest projected impact?
