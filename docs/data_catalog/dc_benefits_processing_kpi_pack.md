## Description

KPI pack for Housing Benefit and Council Tax Support claim processing and change events.

## Business Purpose

Monitor timeliness and quality of benefit administration, with focus on resident impact.

## Data Sources

* Benefits Processing System
* Document Management System
* Customer Contact Centre Data

## Key Measures

* New Claims Processed Within Target (%)
* Change Events Processed Within Target (%)
* Outstanding Work Queue (Cases)
* Decision Accuracy Rate (%)
* Backdated Award Volume

## Questions This Answers

* Are claim backlogs increasing?
* Which process steps drive delays?
* Is decision quality improving?
