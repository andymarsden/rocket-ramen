## Description

Dashboard tracking recycling participation, contamination rates and tonnage trends across wards.

## Business Purpose

Support policy and operational decisions to improve recycling rates and reduce contamination.

## Data Sources

* Materials Recovery Facility reports
* Waste Collection Dataset
* GIS Ward Boundaries

## Key Measures

* Recycling Rate (%)
* Dry Mixed Recycling Tonnage
* Food Waste Capture Rate (%)
* Contamination Rate (%)
* Participation Rate by Ward (%)

## Questions This Answers

* Which wards have low recycling participation?
* Is contamination improving after campaigns?
* How is food waste capture trending?
