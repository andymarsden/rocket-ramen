## Description

## Business Purpose

Identify service pain points and monitor complaints handling against policy standards.

## Data Sources

* CRM Platform
* Contact Centre System
* Directorate Case Management Systems

## Key Measures

* Complaints Received
* Stage 1 Response SLA Compliance (%)
* Escalation Rate (%)
* Upheld Complaints (%)
* Top Complaint Themes

## Questions This Answers

* Which services attract the highest complaint volumes?
* Are complaints resolved within target times?
* What recurring themes indicate systemic issues?
