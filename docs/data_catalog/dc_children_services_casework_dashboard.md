## Description

Dashboard monitoring referral volumes, assessment timeliness and caseload balance across children services teams.

## Business Purpose

Provide operational oversight to maintain safe caseloads and statutory compliance.

## Data Sources

* Children Case Management System
* Multi-Agency Safeguarding Hub Referral Feed
* Workforce Management System

## Key Measures

* Contacts and Referrals Received
* Assessments Completed Within 45 Days (%)
* Child Protection Plan Volumes
* Average Caseload per Practitioner
* Re-Referral Rate (%)

## Questions This Answers

* Are statutory assessment deadlines being met?
* Which teams are carrying unsafe caseloads?
* Are referral and re-referral trends changing?
