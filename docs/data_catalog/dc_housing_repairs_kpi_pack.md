## Description

Monthly KPI pack for responsive repairs, void turnaround and contractor performance in council housing.

## Business Purpose

Track service quality, compliance and value for money in housing repairs delivery.

## Data Sources

* Housing Repairs Management System
* Contractor Job Completion Feeds
* Tenant Contact Centre Data

## Key Measures

* Repairs Completed On Time (%)
* Emergency Repairs Within 24 Hours (%)
* Average Days to Complete Non-Urgent Repairs
* Repeat Repair Rate (%)
* Average Void Turnaround (Days)

## Questions This Answers

* Are repairs meeting contractual SLAs?
* Which repair categories have persistent delays?
* Are tenant outcomes improving over time?
