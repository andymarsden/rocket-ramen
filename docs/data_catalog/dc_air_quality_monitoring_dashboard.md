## Description

Public-facing dashboard presenting real-time and historical local air quality indicators.

## Business Purpose

Inform residents and support policy interventions around emissions, transport and health.

## Data Sources

* Roadside and background monitoring stations
* Defra diffusion tube submissions
* Traffic Flow Dashboard

## Key Measures

* NO2 Annual Mean (ug/m3)
* PM2.5 Annual Mean (ug/m3)
* PM10 Annual Mean (ug/m3)
* Number of Exceedance Days
* AQMA Trend Direction

## Questions This Answers

* Where are pollutant concentrations highest?
* Are levels improving in Air Quality Management Areas?
* How are traffic patterns linked to air quality?
