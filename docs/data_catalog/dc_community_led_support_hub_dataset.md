## Description

Dataset capturing activity through the Community Led Support (CLS) Hub, including first contact triage, strengths-based conversations, community signposting, and short-term support outcomes.

## Business Purpose

Support front-door performance monitoring, prevention reporting, and service redesign by understanding demand, response timeliness, and outcomes from community-led interventions before or instead of long-term care.

## Governance

**Information Asset Owner (IAO):** Head of Adult Social Care Operations  
**System of Record:** Liquidlogic LAS (extract to council data platform nightly)  
**Legal Basis (UK GDPR):** Article 6(1)(e) Public task; Article 9(2)(h) Health or social care management  
**Data Retention:** 7 years after case closure for adult social care case records; derived monitoring extracts retained for 25 months  
**Review Frequency:** Quarterly data quality and access review; annual DPIA and retention policy check  
**Access Control:** Role-based access (CLS practitioners, ASC analysts, service managers) via Azure AD groups with least-privilege permissions  
**Sharing and Disclosure:** Internal ASC performance reporting; external sharing only as aggregated/anonymised outputs unless covered by a formal data sharing agreement  
**Deletion and Disposal:** Monthly retention sweep for expired analytical extracts; secure deletion aligned to council records management standard  
**Auditability:** Create/update/view events logged and retained for 24 months
